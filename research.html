Research

What I work on.

A research portfolio organized around generative vision, multimodal model behavior, 3D visual computing, and optimization.

01 · Areas

Four connected directions

01

Generative AI

Diffusion models, diffusion transformers, GANs, VAEs, controllable generation, image editing, and analogy-based transfer.

02

Vision-Language Models

Multimodal reasoning, consistency-space analysis, logical polarity, and model-specific hallucination signatures.

03

Neural Rendering

NeRF, Gaussian Splatting, 3D reconstruction, camera-aware reasoning, and multi-view visual generation.

04

Optimization

Bayesian optimization, evolutionary search, multi-objective optimization, and diversity-aware generative design.

Research with an implementation bias.
